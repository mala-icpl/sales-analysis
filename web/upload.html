Bring in exports from Vinclum. Upload the SKU Master first (the other two join against it).

1 · SKU Master

The Article → EAN → portal-ID bridge file. Re-uploading updates existing SKUs and adds new ones (nothing is deleted).

Drop or choose the master Excel file (e.g. "all sku master with id's.xlsx")

2 · Inventory Snapshot

Today's stock export from one warehouse. Upload each warehouse separately (same date is fine) — the dashboard combines them automatically. Re-uploading the same date + warehouse replaces just that snapshot.

Drop or choose the inventory CSV/Excel file

3 · Sales

Monthly sales export. The month is detected automatically from the invoice date — re-uploading a month replaces that month's rows, so it's safe to re-pull.

Drop or choose the sales Excel file
